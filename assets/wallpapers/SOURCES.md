# Wallpapers

Featured pictures from Wikimedia Commons, as Commons renders them 2560 pixels wide
(`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/<file>?width=2560`); they aren't changed otherwise.
`slingshot-setup install` copies them next to slingshot.toml, where Settings offers them as the wallpaper,
and makes one of them the wallpaper when slingshot.toml has none.
